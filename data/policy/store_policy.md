# CODEX RETAIL - RETURN, EXCHANGE & WARRANTY POLICY

## 1. General Return Guidelines
At CODEX Retail, we want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. Items eligible for return must be in their original condition, unworn, undamaged, and with all original tags, accessories, and packaging intact. A valid proof of purchase (receipt or order confirmation) is strictly required for all returns and exchanges.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days of receipt inspection.

---

## 2. Return Windows by Product Category

### 2.1 Consumer Electronics & Gadgets
- **Return Window:** 30 calendar days from the date of purchase or delivery.
- **Condition:** Must include all original cords, adapters, manuals, packaging, and free accessories. Serial numbers on the product must match the receipt.
- **Restocking Fee:** A 10% fee may apply if opened without original packaging intact.

### 2.2 Apparel, Clothing & Footwear
- **Return Window:** 30 calendar days from the date of purchase.
- **Condition:** Must be unwashed, unworn, and have all original CODEX tags attached. Footwear must be returned in the original shoebox.
- **Defect Exchange Window:** 90 calendar days for manufacturing defects (zippers, stitching).

### 2.3 Home & Kitchen Appliances
- **Return Window:** 45 calendar days from the date of purchase.
- **Condition:** Cleaned and in working order with all attachments included.

### 2.4 Final Sale & Clearance Items
- **Return Window:** 0 calendar days (FINAL SALE).
- **Policy:** Items marked as "Final Sale", "Clearance", or discounted at 50% or more are strictly non-returnable, non-refundable, and cannot be exchanged.

### 2.5 Personal Care, Health & Perishables
- **Return Window:** 0 calendar days.
- **Policy:** For hygiene and health safety, opened personal audio (in-ear earbuds), health care items, and consumables are strictly non-returnable.

---

## 3. Manufacturer & CODEX Store Warranties

### 3.1 Standard Hardware Warranty (Electronics)
- All new electronics carry a **1-Year (365 days) Limited Hardware Warranty** from CODEX against manufacturer defects in materials and workmanship.
- Does not cover accidental damage, liquid spills, unauthorized modifications, or cosmetic wear and tear.

### 3.2 Premium Appliances Warranty
- Major kitchen and home appliances carry a **2-Year (730 days) Limited Warranty**.

### 3.3 Apparel Warranty
- Apparel carries a **90-day Limited Stitch & Seam Guarantee** covering seam splits and fastener failure under normal usage.

### 3.4 Warranty Claim Process
To initiate a warranty claim, the customer must present their original receipt showing the purchase date, item SKU, and product serial number. CODEX reserves the right to repair, replace with an equivalent unit, or issue store credit if parts are unavailable.
